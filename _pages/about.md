I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at **Yale University**, co-advised by [Mark Gerstein](https://www.gersteinlab.org/) and [Smita Krishnaswamy](https://krishnaswamylab.org/). I develop machine learning methods to understand how biological systems change over time and respond to perturbations.

**Research** &nbsp; My research focuses on **learning dynamics from sparse, high-dimensional observations**. I combine geometry, optimal transport, and generative modeling to infer continuous trajectories, predict responses to new conditions, and understand interactions between cells.

In [**GeoSinkhorn Flow**](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.16.759724v1), we turn static maps of cell populations into models of perturbation responses that generalize to held-out conditions. My work also includes [**ImageFlowNet**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14794), which forecasts disease progression in medical images (ICASSP 2025, Oral), and [**STAGED**](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v311/rocha25a.html), which learns how communication between cells shapes their internal dynamics (MLCB 2025, Oral).

More broadly, I study interpretable models of gene regulation with [chronODE](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-61921-9) (Nature Communications 2025) and the geometry of learned representations with [Dispersion Loss](https://chenliu-1996.github.io/projects/LM-Dispersion/) (ICML 2026).
