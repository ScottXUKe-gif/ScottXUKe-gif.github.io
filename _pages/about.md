I develop machine learning methods for sequential and dynamical data, with a focus on understanding and predicting biological change. I am co-advised by [Mark Gerstein](https://www.gersteinlab.org/) and [Smita Krishnaswamy](https://krishnaswamylab.org/).

My work brings together generative modeling, optimal transport, and representation learning. I am particularly interested in how we can recover dynamics from limited observations, and predict how cells respond to perturbations.
